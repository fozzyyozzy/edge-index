## Surprises and judgment calls

1. **Recency weighting lost.** No-decay (`half_life_games: null`) beat every half-life
   (2/4/8 games) at every λ. Within a season, older games carry as much signal as recent
   ones once the prior is in. H=null is the natural end of the grid, not a clipped edge.
2. **λ landed inside the grid** after I widened it down to 30 (the first grid's best
   was at its lower edge, 100). Stages B and C (per-metric λ, garbage ±1) improved MAE by
   only 0.02 (12.89 → 12.87). Treat those settings as noise-level choices.
3. **`rp_sr` spec won** (rush EPA, pass EPA, success rate). The overall-EPA rating
   (`epa`) is not used by it, so its λ is irrelevant (stage B shows identical MAE).
4. **FCS constant leak, fixed before tuning.** The brief's "FCS mean from 2021-2022"
   would let late-2022 data into early-2022 ratings. Now 2021-2022 use the 2021
   estimate and 2023+ use the 2021-2022 mean. FCS teams are pooled into one pseudo-team
   for that estimate; per-team averages were shrunk to ~0 by ridge. The result is
   ≈ −0.35 net EPA/play, about −24 points vs an average FBS team.
5. **Kneels in older feeds** are written "TEAM run for a loss of N" (2021: 23 plays say
   "kneel" vs 428 TEAM runs). Those are dropped as kneels too.
6. **Pre-snap margin for garbage time** uses the previous play's score, because CFBD
   play scores are post-play. Plays are ordered by drive and play number; play_id
   formats vary by season.
7. **Scrambles:** CFBD tags only about 360 scrambles in 6 seasons, so most scrambles stay
   in rush. Fumbles are assigned rush or pass from the play text; about 1,000 ambiguous
   fumbles count only toward overall EPA and success rate.
8. **Ratings scope:** plays from FBS-involved games only. FCS teams enter through
   their FBS games and shrink to the FCS constant.
9. **Prior fallbacks:** 2022 has no training pairs, so it uses
   prev × 0.6 (`prior_no_history_factor`, fixed, not tuned). 2023 fits that factor on the
   2021→2022 pair. 2024+ use the ridge model. Missing prior features are filled with the
   season's FBS mean. CFBD returning production is offense-only; it gets small positive
   weight on defense too, probably as a program-stability proxy.
10. **Transfer portal net skipped.** Phase 1 has name-matched counts with no quality
    weighting, which isn't a clean team-level measure.
11. **Recency approximation** (unused at the frozen H=null): a play's games-ago comes
    from the offense's schedule only.

## Leakage tests (`engine/cfb/ratings/tests/test_no_leakage.py`)

1. `fit_asof` with every play at/after the 2022 week-6 cutoff set to NaN → all ratings finite.
2. Every play's EPA/success and every game result at/after that cutoff scrambled
   (including all later seasons) → ratings for every as-of week ≤ 6 (and all of 2021) are
   bit-identical. Later weeks do change, so the perturbation is real.
3. All of 2022 scrambled → the 2022 prior is bit-identical.
4. The live entry point (`--as-of`) gives identical output under the same perturbation.

Result: **4 passed**.
